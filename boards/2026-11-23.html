How to read this
  • The big number is our projection if he plays; the blue number under it is our fair line, a coin flip.
  • The ladder shows each line with our fair odds for the over. Tap a line, then type the book's over and under odds.
  • Edge is expected profit per $1 at the book's price. Green means our odds beat theirs.
  • The bars are his last 10 games against the line you picked. Dark bars cleared it.
  • "What if" re-projects the team with that player out. Green and red show the change.
  • Minutes drive everything. If his minutes look wrong (back from injury, new role), trust your read.
